45-minute target · 2–3 people · one keyboard

READINESS
The 45-minute flow below is a design target, not a validated cloud timetable.
The current 30B full-tuning trial took 36m52s and its deployment needs beta
custom-weight access. A smaller-model/local or Serverless AI route is under
discussion. Do not promise that the current Room 2 can finish in 12 minutes.
The earlier checkpoint used the retired dialect format; the revised camera
inputs require a matching model and a measured inference rehearsal.

BEFORE THE STREAM

Test Tavily Extract on that URL. Rehearsal drafts are explicitly marked.
Verify the chosen training and inference route. No new hosting alternative has
been implemented yet. Reset does not cancel jobs or delete endpoints.
