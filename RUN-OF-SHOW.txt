The personality profile changes dialogue, not equipment commands. Training changes
command-model weights. Response settings affect sampling and output length.
Only the caretaker’s measured command plus current safe cooling evidence opens the exit.
Research, model calls, training, serving and cloud execution are real. Broker traffic,
physical equipment and sensor readings are game simulations. Pauses and host experiments
are practice and do not rank. Resetting keeps old UUID saves and releases that game’s
share of the cooling controller; the warm training worker remains allocated until stopped.
